Sticky notes that stay where you put them.

Open Note puts small glass notes on your Windows desktop. They save as you type and are still there after a restart. Free, no account, no ads.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Go on, grab a note. Shake it, throw it, or drop it in the bottom-right corner.

Archived. Nothing is deleted.

What a note can do

The first four work on the notes at the top of this page, so try them there.

Star what matters
Click the star in the corner. Starred notes go to the top of your list.
Shake it to see behind
Shake a note while you hold it and it fades, so you can read what's underneath. Let go and it comes back.
Throw it out of the way
Fling a note and it glides, bouncing off the edges of the screen.
Drop it in the bin
Drag a note to the bottom-right corner to archive it. Nothing is deleted; restore it from the notes list.
Write the way you think
Checklists, bullets, bold and italic, and screenshots pasted straight in.
Hear it read aloud
Any line read out with the Windows voices you already have. Works offline.
Check your English
Optional: get a corrected and a more natural version of a sentence, or turn Vietnamese into English, with your own free Google Gemini key.
Stays out of the way
Lives in the notification area and starts with Windows. About 44 MB of memory, no CPU while idle, and your notes never leave your PC.

One shortcut away, from any app

Forgot one? Press Ctrl+Alt+/ and a card with all of them appears in the middle of the screen.

Anywhere
CtrlAltNNew note by the mouse pointer
CtrlAltHHide or show every note
CtrlAltPChoose which notes stay on screen
CtrlAltMAll notes, search and archive
CtrlAlt/How to use
CtrlAltQQuit Open Note completely
In a note
CtrlEnterMake a checklist item, or tick it
CtrlB CtrlIBold and italic
CtrlShiftSStar the note
CtrlRRead aloud
CtrlGCheck with AI
CtrlDArchive the note

Get Open Note

  1. Download the installer

    One file with everything inside. You don't need to install anything else.

    Download for Windows

  2. Run it

    If Windows SmartScreen says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. That appears because the app isn't code-signed yet.

  3. Write your first note

    Press CtrlAltN. From now on Open Note starts with Windows; you can turn that off in its tray menu.
